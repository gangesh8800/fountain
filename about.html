Our story

A founder-led fountain company, built on 15 years in the trade — one team from design to aftercare.

Global Water Fountains Enterprises is led by its founder, with 15 years of experience in making water fountains. We design, install and repair fountains for homes, hotels and public spaces — and we do it with our own team, not outside contractors.

Our workshop is in Shahdara, Delhi — our projects are not. We design, deliver and install across India, and we travel for surveys on larger projects.

That is how we work: we listen first, suggest honestly what suits your space and your budget, and build it properly — with waterproofing and plumbing done right from the start, and equipment chosen for years of daily running.

After handover, we stay a phone call away. Servicing, repairs and annual maintenance, whenever you need us.
