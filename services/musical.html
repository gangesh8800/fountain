Musical Fountains

Water that moves with music — choreographed jets, colour and sound, programmed into a show people gather to watch.

What it includes

  • Choreographed jets, colour lighting and sound
  • Programming for a show people gather to watch
  • Controls, installation and programming by our own team
  • Aftercare and servicing after handover

Ideal for

Public spaces, hotels, resorts, malls and city plazas.

How it works

We start with a call or WhatsApp message about your space. For larger projects, we travel to survey the site in person. You get a design and an itemised quotation, so you can see exactly what you are paying for — then our own team builds it, starts it with you, and stays in touch afterwards.
